About

My work sits at the intersection of neural signals, machine intelligence, and human health.

I am a researcher in Computer Science and Computational Neuroscience, currently pursuing my PhD at Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology (MNNIT), Allahabad.

I focus on EEG signal analysis and deep-learning frameworks for cognitive-state detection, neurological-disorder analysis, sleep staging, and low-power wearable sensing.
