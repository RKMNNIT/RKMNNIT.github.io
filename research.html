About

My work sits at the intersection of neural signals, machine intelligence, and human health.

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science and Engineering at Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology (MNNIT) Allahabad, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computational neuroscience, and neurophysiological signal processing.

My primary focus is EEG-based modelling of cognitive and brain states using robust, interpretable, physiology-guided, and computationally efficient learning. Broader interests include multimodal neurophysiological signals, temporal representation learning, brain-computer interfaces, human movement, and AI-enabled neurorehabilitation.

Research

Research agenda

From neural signals
to dependable decisions.

Three core themes organize my work: understanding brain dynamics, designing interpretable learning systems, and making those systems practical beyond the lab. I also explore EEG-informed human movement and neurorehabilitation.

01

Computational neuroscience & BCI

EEG-based modeling of cognitive and neurological states, with an emphasis on physiologically meaningful representations and multimodal signals.

  • Cognitive state analysis
  • Multimodal neurophysiological signals
  • Brain–computer interfaces
  • Human movement & neurorehabilitation
02

Interpretable health AI

Deep-learning systems for sleep staging and clinical analysis that expose evidence, uncertainty, and physiological structure rather than acting as black boxes.

  • Sleep-stage classification
  • Explainable AI
  • Clinical decision support
03

Trustworthy edge intelligence

Resource-efficient, privacy-aware learning for wearable and consumer-health systems, including federated learning and auditable machine unlearning.

  • Wearable sensing
  • Edge-efficient models
  • Privacy-preserving learning
04

Human movement & neurorehabilitation

EEG-informed modelling of human movement and locomotor adaptation, with broader interest in AI-enabled and robot-assisted neurorehabilitation.

  • Human movement
  • Locomotor adaptation
  • Robot-assisted rehabilitation
