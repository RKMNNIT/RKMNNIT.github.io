Research

Research agenda.

My work centres on EEG and multimodal neurophysiological signals, including cognitive-state modelling, meditation and devotional practices, physiological interpretability, cross-subject generalisation, computational efficiency, and human-centred neurotechnology.

01

Computational neuroscience & BCI

EEG-based modelling of cognitive and neurological states, with an emphasis on physiologically meaningful representations and multimodal neurophysiological signals.

  • Cognitive state analysis
  • Multimodal neurophysiological signals
  • Brain–computer interfaces
02

Meditation & devotional-practice EEG

EEG-based investigation of neural dynamics during mantra meditation and devotional practices, including spectral–temporal signatures, hemispheric engagement, and state-dependent brainwave patterns.

  • Mantra meditation
  • Devotional practices
  • Spectral–temporal EEG
03

Interpretable health AI

Deep-learning systems for sleep staging and clinical analysis that expose evidence, uncertainty, and physiological structure rather than acting as black boxes.

  • Sleep-stage classification
  • Explainable AI
  • Physiology-guided modelling
04

Trustworthy edge intelligence

Resource-efficient and privacy-aware learning for wearable and consumer-health systems, including federated learning, model auditing, and deployment under constrained sensing environments.

  • Wearable sensing
  • Edge-efficient models
  • Privacy-preserving learning
05

Human movement & neurorehabilitation

EEG-informed modelling of human movement and locomotor adaptation, with broader interest in AI-enabled and robot-assisted neurorehabilitation.

  • Human movement
  • Locomotor adaptation
  • Neurorehabilitation
